# 🌊 Disruption Dynamics — COVID Impact on Marine Health

## 🧭 Problem Framing & Decision Context
COVID-related activity reductions provided a natural experiment to examine ecosystem responses. Understanding how reduced industrial and agricultural pressures affect marine health can guide policymakers and environmental agencies in scenario planning.

This module compares pre- vs post-COVID marine health, integrating **lagged human pressures** to identify areas of improvement or decline.

## 📘 Executive Summary
- Input: EcoPulse Index per tissue, industrial & agricultural activity
- Processing: temporal comparison with lagged pressures
- Output: pre/post-COVID maps, trend plots, dynamic insights

**Key insights**
- Some regions show improvement in EcoPulse where human activity declined
- Others remain stressed due to legacy pollution or persistent pressures

**Takeaway for decision-makers:** identify sensitive regions, plan interventions, and understand ecosystem responsiveness.

## 🌍 Real-World Value
- Scenario-based policy guidance
- Highlights lagged effects of human activity on ecosystems
- Supports monitoring, research, and resource allocation

## 🎯 Applied Use Cases
- Policy evaluation: measure ecosystem response to human activity reduction
- Targeted monitoring and restoration
- Integration with predictive modeling for future scenarios

## 🧩 Module Overview

**Core Objectives**
- Compare pre- vs post-COVID EcoPulse scores
- Incorporate industrial & agricultural pressures with lag
- Generate maps, trend plots, and plain-language insights

**Outputs Generated**
- Interactive maps of EcoPulse pre/post-COVID
- Time-series plots of human pressures
- Dynamic insights section for non-technical interpretation

**Interactive Features**
- Select tissue type and years
- Include/exclude transition year 2020
- Insights section explains trends in plain language
